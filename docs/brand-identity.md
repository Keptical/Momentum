# Momentum Brand Identity

## App Name

Momentum

---

## Tagline

Build momentum. Every day.

---

## Mission

Help people build lasting consistency across fitness, productivity, and personal growth through one simple app.

---

## Vision

To become the daily app people open to improve themselves—not because they have to, but because they want to.

---

## Core Values

- Simplicity
- Consistency
- Progress
- Transparency

---

## Design Principles

- Dark-first experience
- Minimalistic interface
- Fast interactions
- Motivating, not overwhelming
- Progress over perfection

---

## Target Audience

Students, professionals, and anyone looking to build better routines.

---

## Brand Personality

Momentum should feel:

- Motivating
- Modern
- Clean
- Reliable
- Encouraging

Not:

- Loud
- Cluttered
- Guilt-inducing
- Distracting

---

## Logo Direction

Simple geometric icon.
Recognisable at small sizes.
Works in monochrome.
